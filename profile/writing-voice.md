# Writing voice for cover letters

Based on Fenjo's BMW cover letter (`example_cover_letter_BMW.pdf`). Follow these rules unless he says otherwise.

## Structure
1. **Opening with substance, not a formula.** Start with the question or problem the team works on and why it has occupied him. Then one sentence on why he wants to contribute to exactly this team. No "hiermit bewerbe ich mich".
2. **Academic paragraph.** Current studies, then one concrete finding or argument from his work (usually the thesis), then how the rest of his studies connect to the role. Name specific programs and events instead of general claims.
3. **Practical paragraph.** Show skills through what he did (Diplomacy Society, Democracy Summit, hotel job if relevant). Claim a skill, then prove it in the same paragraph.
4. **Why this organization.** One or two sentences that could only be written for this employer.
5. **Availability and close.** Start date, hours (for Werkstudent roles), wish for a personal conversation.

## Tone
- Confident and direct, without overselling. "Ich würde gerne dazu beitragen", not "Ich bin der perfekte Kandidat".
- Analytical. He likes to show a real opinion or conclusion.
- Formal but not stiff. German letters use "Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren" or the named contact, and "Mit freundlichen Grüßen".

## Rules
- One page. The BMW letter ran close to the limit with very long paragraphs, so aim for 3 to 4 paragraphs of moderate length with a blank line between them.
- No em dashes and no semicolons.
- No invented facts. Use only `cv-master.md` and `proof-points.md`.
- The school is called **Hertie School** (not "Hertie School of Governance", which is the old name).
- Match the language of the posting (German or English).
- In German, use the gender form the employer uses in the posting.
