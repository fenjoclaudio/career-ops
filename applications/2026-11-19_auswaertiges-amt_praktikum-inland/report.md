## Hard filter check
- Must be a Pflichtpraktikum confirmed by the university. Unclear whether the Hertie Professional Year qualifies. Check with Hertie Career Development.
- Main target group are German citizens (Art. 116 GG). Citizenship is not stated in profile/. Confirm.
- Full-time only, 6 weeks to 3 months. Only possible outside the semester.
- Pay: 450 EUR per month allowance.
- Not selected applicants hear two months before the requested start.

## Fit summary
This is the program his whole search points at. It is a standing program, not a single posting, so the real deadline is the 6-month lead time. For an August or September 2027 start, the application must be in by early February or March 2027. The bottleneck is paperwork: the mandatory internship confirmation comes first in the single PDF.

## Strongest arguments for Fenjo
- Five languages, lived in four countries, which the AA lists as selection criteria (non-tourist stays abroad, languages)
- Strong grades (CBS 1.8 equivalent, GWU 3.74, top 5% European Baccalaureate)
- Coursework in national security and contemporary diplomacy, China, geoeconomics

## Gaps and risks
- Mandatory internship confirmation from Hertie is needed
- Abitur certificate and grade overview needed in the PDF
- Max 3 months, so it covers only part of the Professional Year
- High competition

## Recommendation
Apply, high priority, with caveats. Ask Hertie now whether the Professional Year can be confirmed as a Pflichtpraktikum, and apply via the portal by early February 2027.

## Angle for the cover letter
The portal application relies on documents more than a letter. Where free text is allowed, connect his multilingual European background and his work on EU critical raw materials and economic security to a concrete unit (for example economic security, Europe or China units).
