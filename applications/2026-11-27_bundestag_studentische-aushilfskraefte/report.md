## Hard filter check
- Deadline: none, standing call
- Hours: compatible with the MIA
- Requirements: must be enrolled full-time at a university in Berlin or Brandenburg (Hertie qualifies as state-recognized, worth confirming). **No other side job allowed**, which rules out combining it with a second Werkstudent role
- Pay: TVöD EG 3, about 1,440 EUR gross at 19.5 h/week (Verwaltung)

## Fit summary
A standing call for student staff in the Bundestag administration. It gives an inside view of parliament and solid pay, but the work is mostly visitor guiding or general admin, and the applicant cannot pick the unit. The international relations and Europe units would be interesting, but there is no guarantee of placement there. The no-side-job rule makes it an either-or choice against a better Werkstudent role.

## Strongest arguments for Fenjo
- Native German speaker with C2 English and Italian, an asset for the Besucherdienst
- Studies international affairs in Berlin, a natural fit for the international relations or Europe units

## Gaps and risks
- Unit allocation is not in his hands, likely Besucherdienst
- No other side job allowed
- Long document list (enrollment, certificates, ID, German proof, two forms)

## Recommendation
Apply only as a fallback, or if no stronger Werkstudent role for January comes through. If he applies, state a clear preference for the international relations or Europe units.

## Angle for the cover letter
Lead with his interest in how parliament shapes foreign and European policy, and ask explicitly for the Verwaltung track in international relations or Europe. Mention his languages as a plus for visitor groups as a second option.
