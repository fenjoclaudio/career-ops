# Scoring a posting

Score each criterion from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). Weighted score = sum of (score / 5 × weight). Maximum 100.

## Criteria

| # | Criterion | Weight | What a 5 looks like | What a 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reputation** of the organization | 25 | Top-tier name in German or international foreign policy (Auswärtiges Amt, Kanzleramt, SWP, McKinsey) | Unknown or weak reputation |
| 2 | **Network value** of the role | 25 | Daily contact with senior people in foreign or security policy, alumni who move into ministries and top think tanks | Isolated back-office role, little contact beyond the immediate team |
| 3 | **Path to foreign policy** | 15 | Directly builds toward a career in the Auswärtiges Amt or foreign and security policy | No link to that path |
| 4 | **Content fit** | 15 | Foreign, security, geoeconomic, European or strategic policy work with real analytical tasks | Pure admin, event logistics or unrelated subject matter |
| 5 | **Format and timing** | 10 | Matches the phase exactly (see below), Berlin, German or English | Wrong city, wrong timing, wrong format |
| 6 | **Chance of success** | 10 | He meets all requirements and has a clear edge | Hard requirements he does not meet |

### Format and timing by phase
- **Start Jan 2027 to Jul 2027:** Werkstudent = 5. Part-time internship = 4. Full-time internship during the semester = 1 (conflicts with the MIA).
- **Start Aug/Sep 2027 onward:** full-time role suitable for the Professional Year = 5.
- Outside Berlin and not remote = at most 2.

## Grades
| Score | Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 85 to 100 | A | Apply, high priority |
| 70 to 84 | B | Apply |
| 55 to 69 | C | Apply only if it fills a gap or there is spare time |
| 40 to 54 | D | Probably skip |
| below 40 | F | Skip |

## Hard filters (flag clearly, regardless of score)
- Deadline already passed
- Hours not compatible with the MIA during the semester
- Requirements he cannot meet (for example, a finished master's degree, a specific citizenship, a security clearance he cannot get in time, a language he does not speak)
- Unpaid full-time role (flag, do not automatically reject)

## Notes
- Do not inflate scores because the name is famous. Reputation already has its own weight.
- If information is missing (hours, pay, start date), score conservatively and say what is unknown.
- Pay is not a criterion for now. Mention it in the report if it is known.
